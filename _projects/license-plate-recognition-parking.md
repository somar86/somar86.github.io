**Role:** Designer and developer · **Organization:** UET Lahore (BS Electrical Engineering capstone, 2009)

## Overview

I built an **end-to-end, computer-vision based license-plate recognition (LPR) system** that automates a parking lot, from capturing vehicle images at the gate to identifying the vehicle and managing entry and exit with no human attendant.

## The problem

Manual parking management is slow, labor-intensive, and error-prone: attendants issue tickets, log vehicles, and verify exits by hand, which causes queues at peak times and makes record keeping unreliable.

## Approach

- **Image acquisition** at entry and exit points
- **Plate localization** to find the license plate in the vehicle image
- **Character segmentation and recognition** to read the plate number
- **Parking management logic** that logs vehicles, matches entries to exits, and controls access automatically

## Impact

- A complete, working automated parking system, from camera to gate control
- Early hands-on work in applied AI and computer vision that grew into a career in AI and computational science
