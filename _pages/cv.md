Track record of leading end-to-end AI/ML portfolios, from ideation and conception to model deployment and monitoring with CI/CD, integrated with digital twins and operational workflows. 20+ patents, peer-reviewed publications, and cross-functional leadership spanning data scientists and domain experts; recognized by the US National Academy of Engineering.

## Experience

*Cambridge, MA, USA · Nov. 2023 – present*

- **Transformers and diffusion models:** Applying novel architectures to real-time processing of [3D inversions of ultra-deep resistivity measurements](/projects/udar-3d-inversion/).
